TechBridge: IT & Cybersecurity Training

Components:
Free or low-cost training in software development, cloud computing, and digital marketing.
Hands-on projects, internship opportunities, and job placement support.
Special focus on bridging the digital divide for low-income communities.
Impact: Increases employment opportunities in the tech industry and promotes digital inclusion.

Financial Freedom & Entrepreneurship Academy

​Focus: Teach financial literacy and entrepreneurship skills to help individuals achieve economic stability and independence.
Components:
Budgeting, credit management, and debt reduction workshops.
Training on business planning, marketing, and securing funding for small businesses.
Mentorship from local entrepreneurs and business leaders.
Impact: Empowers individuals to start businesses, build savings, and improve financial health.

Youth Cricket & Wellness Program  

The Guardian Angels Youth Cricket & Wellness Program www.AmericaPlaysCricket.org represents an opportunity to empower youth, promote health, and strengthen community bonds in Morrisville and Bucks County.
This initiative will provide accessible, structured, and impactful programming for 200 youth annually, helping them grow both on and off the field.
