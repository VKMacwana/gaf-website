OUR MISSION

At Guardian Angels, we are a Christ-centered organization dedicated to extending love and care to those in need. Through compassion and grace, we promote sustainable change by advancing environmental stewardship, empowering women, and enriching education—restoring dignity and hope in every life we touch.

​✏️🎒 Back-to-School Blessings! ¡Bendiciones de Regreso a Clases! 📚✨

​Aug 27, 2025

🎒📚 Over 400 school supplies delivered to bilingual & neurodiverse 2nd-grade students at Carroll Robbins Elementary School, Trenton (New Jersey Public Schools) (estudiantes con necesidades de aprendizaje diversas) 🙌💛

Back-to-School Blessings!

Dropped off at Village Park Academy (Pennsbury School District, Bucks County, Pennsylvania  ) in partnership with Pennsbury Kids Organization - 190 packs of markers, 120 packs of colored pencils, 510 glue sticks, 200 scissors, and 200 highlighters. More than 500 students will benefit as they start the school year strong!
Huge thanks to our partner in ministry, Peace Zones, Inc., and to every volunteer, donor & prayer partners who made this possible. All glory to God!

🌿 Project Green Roots: Plant Today, Breathe Tomorrow 🌱
Welcome to our tree plantation initiative—an effort rooted in hope and driven by action. Through every sapling we plant, we’re restoring balance, reviving ecosystems, and building a legacy of green for generations to come.
"The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago. The second-best time is now." –  Proverb
This project is more than planting trees—it's about growing a movement. Together, let’s turn empty spaces into living forests and inspire communities to protect our planet.
