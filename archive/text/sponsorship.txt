Become a Sponsor

Guardian Angels invites you to help make a difference in the lives of needy people by becoming a Corporate Sponsor. Your contribution is needed to help grow and expand programs that provide help and hope to these people.

Your generous support of the people in our local area is making a difference every day in our community. By investing in them, you are investing in the future.

WHEN YOUR COMPANY SUPPORTS GUARDIAN ANGELS:

It sends a strong message to the community that your organization is socially responsible and cares about giving back to the community. It’s a win/win!
It demonstrates to your employees that your company is forward-thinking and committed to making a difference in the community where they live. Your employees can take advantage of many volunteer opportunities that directly benefit children in need.
Volunteering as a group encourages team building, pride in the organization, and the satisfaction of giving back whether through funds or time. The organization and employees gain a better understanding of the challenges facing the community in which they live, work, and support.
These are just a few of the ways in which your company or organization will benefit from a partnership with Guardian Angels. A relationship with Guardian Angels is more than just a business deal; it’s a promise to our community for a better tomorrow.
