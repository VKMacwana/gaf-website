Get Involved

You’re invited to make a difference with us.

Whether you’re passionate about mentoring students, serving your community, advocating for justice, or supporting people in need — there’s a place for you here. When you get involved, you don’t just give your time — you become part of a movement rooted in love, service, and impact.

Volunteer

Bring your skills, energy, and heart to one of our ongoing projects or events. From mentoring youth to helping with community outreach or logistics at conferences, your time makes a real difference.

Sign Up to Volunteer

Become a Mentor

Support students, especially international and faith-based students, as they navigate education and life in North America. You don’t need to have all the answers — just a listening ear and a willing heart.

Your generosity helps us run our programs, support students, and respond to real community needs — from refugee assistance to health campaigns. Every gift, big or small, counts.
