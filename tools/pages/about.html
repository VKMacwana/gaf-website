About Guardian Angels

A Christ-centered organization committed to cultivating a sustainable and compassionate future.

Mission Statement

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: meet real needs with real love. From hygiene kits and school supplies to tree plantings and mentorship, we show up where communities need us most — across the United States, India, and Canada — guided by faith and driven by compassion.

“For He will command His angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways.”

Psalm 91:11

Who We Are

Rooted in Faith, Inspired by the Teachings of Jesus

Guardian Angels Foundation is a Christ-centered nonprofit dedicated to service rooted in Scripture and compassion.

We believe in honoring God’s creation, uplifting the dignity of women and girls, and unlocking the transformative power of education. Our work is prayerfully guided and purpose-driven.

We walk by faith, not by sight — trusting that every seed we plant, every woman we empower, and every child we educate brings us closer to a world that reflects God’s love and justice.

What Guides Us

Three Pillars, One Mission

Through compassion and grace, we promote sustainable change — restoring dignity and hope in every life we touch.

Environmental Stewardship

Honoring God’s creation — planting trees, restoring ecosystems, and building a legacy of green for generations to come.

Women’s Empowerment

Uplifting the dignity of women and girls with hygiene education, essential resources, and practical skills training.

Educational Enrichment

Unlocking the transformative power of education through mentorship, resources, and opportunity for underserved youth.

Be Part of the Story

Whether through a gift, your time, or your prayers — every act of love moves this mission forward.
